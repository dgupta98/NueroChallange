# Job Skills & Employment Platforms for Neurodiverse Adults
**Compiled: Oct 27 | Live research + transcript analysis**

> Covers every major platform for teaching job-ready skills to neurodivergent adults (autistic, ADHD, IDD, dyslexia). Organized by type: tech training, vocational, corporate hiring programs, soft skills, and the unmet gap.

---

## The Big Picture: Three Separate Problems

People often conflate these but they're distinct:

| Phase | Problem | Who's Solving It | How Well |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pre-hire: skills** | Don't have the skills for a job | Aspiritech, Specialisterne, Coding Autism, Fidgetech | Moderately well — growing ecosystem |
| **Pre-hire: matching** | Have skills but can't navigate hiring | Mentra, SAP/Microsoft programs, auticon | Partially — mostly tech jobs only |
| **Post-hire: retention** | Got the job, can't keep it | **Nobody** (Georgia Tech research, not a product) | **Not solved** |

First Place AZ's gap — and what Josh described — lives in the third column.

**Programs (live as of Oct 2026):**

| QA Testing (Beginner) | 6 weeks | DHS Trusted Tester | $4,200 |
| Digital Accessibility | 6 weeks | DHS Trusted Tester | $4,200 |
| QA + Accessibility (Full Path) | 12 weeks | Both | $8,000 |
| IT Foundations — Beginner | 6 weeks | CompTIA Tech+ | $4,500 |

**Relevance to First Place AZ:** Aspiritech's programs align directly to First Place's employer partners — tech roles (AMC, U-Haul IT), accessibility roles (any corporate partner), marketing. ### Specialisterne USA Academy ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Free IT Training

**What it is:** 8-week fully online IT training program for autistic job seekers, completely free.
- Technical AND soft skill instruction
- Explicitly designed for people interested in IT careers
- Run by Specialisterne USA (Forbes Accessibility 200, 2026)
- **Funded by HG Foundation partnership** — no cost to participants
- Applicants do not need to live in a specific state

**Parent org (Specialisterne):** Originally founded in Denmark. The model: leverage autistic people's pattern recognition, attention to detail, and systematic thinking for tech roles. Now in 13+ countries. Named by Forbes as one of 200 leading global accessibility innovators.

**National partnership with The Precisionists, Inc.** (see below)

**What it misses:** Training only — no direct employer connection after completion for everyone. No post-hire support. Primarily IT-focused, not hospitality/service industry (where many First Place residents work).

### Coding Autism ⭐⭐⭐

**What it is:** 20-week, full-time coding bootcamp specifically for autistic adults.
- Teaches fundamentals needed to secure a junior developer job
- Available via Republic.com (investor crowdfunding model)

**What it misses:** Long (20 weeks full-time is a major commitment). No post-hire support. Not yet at scale.

### Fidgetech ⭐⭐⭐

**What it is:** Nonprofit offering virtual training in coding, design, and AI for adults experiencing autism.
- Neurodivergent-friendly design throughout
- Virtual delivery

**What it misses:** Smaller program, limited scale data available. Pre-hire focus.

---

### auticon ⭐⭐⭐⭐ — Employment Model, Not Training

**What it is:** IT consulting firm that hires exclusively autistic professionals. Not a training program — they hire and then place autistic consultants at client companies.
- US operations (auticon US) + global presence
- Clients include major corporations
- Autistic employees get ongoing job coaching as part of employment (not just pre-hire)
- **This is one of the few models with built-in post-hire support** — job coaches are part of the employment model, not a separate program

**Why this matters:** auticon's model demonstrates that post-hire job coaching is viable and scalable when built into the employment structure. The gap for First Place residents is that their employers (AMC, Zoo, Aramark) are NOT auticon — they don't have built-in coaches.

## Category B: Corporate Neurodiversity Hiring Programs

### SAP Autism at Work ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ — The Gold Standard

**Stats:**
- Started: 2013
- Scale: 240+ autistic employees in 16 countries
- **92% retention rate** (vs. ~50% in general workforce)
- Roles: Software engineering, data science, quality assurance, content writing, product design, accessibility

**How hiring works:**
- Work-tryout instead of interview: candidates spend ~1 week doing actual sample work tasks
- Job coach available before AND during interviews for those who want one
- No ambiguous behavioral interview questions

**How retention works:**
- Focused onboarding support
- Job coaches + mentor buddy system (one neurotypical, one autistic mentor)
- Persistent accommodation support throughout tenure
- Manager training on neurodivergent communication

**What makes the 92% retention work:** It's not the hiring process — it's the ongoing structure. Mentors, coaches, clear communication norms, and manager awareness don't stop after 90 days.

**Relevance to First Place:** SAP's retention practices are exactly what's missing for First Place's employer partners (AMC, Zoo, Aramark). Those employers don't have job coaches, mentor buddies, or manager training. The gap your product fills is providing the support layer that SAP builds in-house — but for employers who aren't SAP.

## Category E: Soft Skills & Workplace Communication (The Retention Gap)

### What Exists

**Autism Working Programme (UK):**
- Training for workplace managers on supporting autistic staff
- Webcast-based training
- **Staff-facing, not worker-facing**

**Sachsen Center / NDC Social Skills Training:**
- Communication skills training for neurodivergent adults
- Evidence-based, clinician-delivered
- Pre-hire prep — not in-the-moment workplace support

**EZducate AI (from earlier research):**
- Workplace social scripts, ADA accommodation templates
- Therapist/job coach productivity tool
- Not resident-facing

**Meristem / Vocational Program Sites:**
- General guidance on balancing hard + soft skills
- No product — advice/information only

### What Does NOT Exist

This is the clearest gap in the entire landscape:

> A digital tool that provides **in-the-moment, real-time soft skills support** for a neurodivergent worker who is **currently employed** and faces a workplace situation they don't know how to handle.

Scenarios that have no product solution:
- "My manager said my work was 'not quite right' — what does that mean and how do I respond?"
- "The schedule changed without warning and I'm overwhelmed at work right now"
- "I made a mistake and my coworker seems upset — what do I do?"
- "I have my 90-day review in 2 days and don't know what to expect"
- "I've been at AMC for 6 months and nobody told me what I'm doing wrong or right"

**Georgia Tech NSF Research ($500K, Jan 2026):** The closest thing to this gap. Jennifer Kim (HCI) + Mark Riedl (AI/ML) building LLMs for job coaching — but explicitly identified post-probationary period as the key gap, and it's still research-phase, not a product.

## The Learning Modality Research

### What Works for Neurodiverse Learners (Evidence Base)

| Modality | Works Well For | Struggles With |
|---|---|---|
| **Video (self-paced)** | Autism, ADHD — can pause/replay, no social pressure | Long videos without interaction |
| **Gamified micro-learning** | ADHD — dopamine loops, immediate reward | Needs consistent design, not novelty-overloaded |
| **Text + visual (dual coding)** | Dyslexia, ID — visual supports comprehension | Text-only is inaccessible |
| **Simulation/VR** | Autism — predictable practice environment | High setup cost, clinician dependency |
| **Body doubling / live cohort** | ADHD — accountability structure | Social anxiety for some autistic learners |
| **Checklist / step breakdown** | ID, ADHD — concrete task structure | Abstract goals without steps fail |

**Goblin.tools** (goblin.tools) — AI task decomposition tool actively used in ND communities. Input: one task. Output: step-by-step breakdown with time estimates. Free. No login. Explicit ADHD/autism design. This is the UX reference for "break this skill into steps."

## What This All Means for the Build

### The Landscape in One Sentence
The pre-hire skills ecosystem is growing (Aspiritech, Specialisterne, Coding Autism, SAP). The post-hire retention infrastructure for service-sector jobs doesn't exist anywhere.

### Where Your Product Fits

```
[EXISTING]                          [YOUR PRODUCT]                    [EXISTING]
Gateway CC courses         →        Skill milestone tracker     →     TPI / Aspiritech placement
First Place Be Open Be Ready  →     Progress + AI next steps    →     AMC / Zoo / Aramark job
Transition Academy         →        Confidence + self-direction →     Independence at Thomas at Midtown
```

Your product is the longitudinal thread that connects First Place's existing training infrastructure to the employment outcomes they're trying to achieve — while also being the in-the-moment support layer for workers once they're placed.

### Two Product Layers Worth Building

**Layer 1 (10-day MVP): Skill Milestone Tracker**
- Resident-owned, not staff-owned
- Life skill domains from First Place's 360 framework (Physical, Social, Financial, Vocational, Mental)
- AI suggests next skills based on current progress
- Can share progress with family / support team
- "You've attended cooking class 4 times — you're ready to host"

**Layer 2 (post-hackathon / pitch vision): In-the-Moment Job Coach**
- Triggered by a resident who hits a workplace situation they can't navigate
- "My manager said X — what does that mean?"
- Context-aware to their specific employer (AMC scripts ≠ Zoo scripts)
- Connects to skill milestones ("navigating ambiguous feedback" = a skill you're building)

### The Pitch Narrative

> "SAP has 92% retention for autistic employees — because they built job coaches, mentors, and manager training into the employment structure. AMC Theaters and the Phoenix Zoo don't have that. First Place residents are getting jobs and then losing them because the support falls off. We're building the layer SAP has in-house — for every employer, accessible to every First Place resident from their phone."
