# Judging Rubric

**Organizer:** Spark Center for Innovation in Learning  
**Total Points:** 50 (10 criteria × 5-point scale each)  
**Source:** 2026 NA AI Challenge Rubric Overview

---

## 10 Judging Criteria

| # | Criterion | What It Evaluates | Key Question |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **MVP Development** | Progress, usability, and completeness of the prototype | How well-developed and functional is the team's MVP in demonstrating the solution's core value? |
| 2 | **User-Centered Problem** | Clarity and empathy in defining the problem through neurodiversity/executive function lens | How clearly and thoughtfully does the team define the problem and user persona, especially re: autism, dyslexia, ADHD, or mental health? |
| 3 | **Impact & Inclusivity** | How the solution advances accessibility, empowerment, or opportunity for neurodivergent learners | How well does the solution demonstrate or anticipate meaningful impact for neurodivergent users through inclusive design and responsible AI use? |
| 4 | **Functionality** | Whether the demo achieves its intended purpose | Does the prototype function as intended and effectively demonstrate its core features? |
| 5 | **Collaboration** | Teamwork, complementary skills, and inclusive practices | How effectively did the team collaborate, leverage diverse strengths, and create an inclusive working environment? |
| 6 | **Principled Innovation** | How meaningfully and responsibly AI/advanced tech is integrated | Was AI (or other advanced tech) applied in a way that enhances accessibility, personalization, or learning outcomes? |
| 7 | **Creativity & Vision** | Originality and forward-thinking potential | How original, imaginative, or visionary is the team's solution in addressing neurodivergent learning challenges? |
| 8 | **Tools & Techniques** | Quality and appropriateness of technical and creative approaches | Were the chosen tools, methods, and technologies appropriate, well-integrated, and effectively applied? |
| 9 | **Pitch Storytelling** | Clarity, engagement, and adherence to time limits | Was the pitch well-structured, engaging, and within the allotted time? |
| 10 | **Growth & Scalability** | Long-term feasibility and real-world potential | Does the project show clear potential for growth, adoption, or broader societal impact? |

---

## What Jurors Need to Understand

1. The need is **real, specific, and supported** by Challenge Partner research
2. The AI capability is **purposeful, feasible, and meaningfully different** from a generic productivity tool
3. The MVP **works** and demonstrates the proposed value
4. The team has considered **ethics, privacy, accessibility, user agency, and technical limits**
5. The venture has a **credible audience, adoption path, and potential to scale responsibly**

---

## Scoring Tips Per Criterion

### MVP Development
- Show a working prototype, not a mockup
- Core features must function end-to-end during the demo
- AI must be demonstrably integrated, not simulated

### User-Centered Problem
- Name a specific user persona (e.g., "a 9th-grade student with ADHD at AZACS")
- Connect problem to real observations from Partner research
- Tie back to executive function specifically

### Impact & Inclusivity
- Explain who benefits and how
- Show inclusive design decisions (accessibility, AAC-friendly, sensory considerations)
- Address responsible AI use explicitly

### Functionality
- Test thoroughly before pitch
- Have a pre-recorded demo as backup
- Ensure the demo path works on the presentation device

### Collaboration
- Be ready to discuss each member's contribution
- Show interdisciplinary team strengths (tech + business + domain)

### Principled Innovation
- Explain WHY AI is used (not just that it is)
- Address bias, privacy, and user agency
- Show values-based decision-making

### Creativity & Vision
- Show what makes your solution different from existing tools
- Demonstrate imagination grounded in research

### Tools & Techniques
- Justify technology choices
- Show they're appropriate for the user context and neurodivergent users
- Code quality matters (code review happens Nov. 7–9)

### Pitch Storytelling
- Practice hitting 5 minutes exactly — slides auto-advance
- Structure: Team → User → Problem → Solution+Demo → Next Steps
- Engage the jury, don't just read slides

### Growth & Scalability
- Address adoption path: how do users find/use it?
- Address scale: what's the potential reach?
- Address sustainability: how does it continue to exist?
