## Additional Benefits for 1st Place (Gold)

- **Automatic entry into the Global AI Challenge** hosted by ASU's J. Orin Edson Entrepreneurship + Innovation Institute (emerging track for pre-seed and seed founders)
- Access to mentorship and technical support from Arizona State University
- Engagement with a global community of innovators tackling similar challenges
- Entry into SCIL's virtual **Pitch Studio** to prepare for live pitching opportunities
- Platform to spotlight work and learn from leaders in AI, entrepreneurship, and edtech
- **Year-long support from SCIL's virtual Venture Studio** — refine business model, validate impact, prepare for investors
