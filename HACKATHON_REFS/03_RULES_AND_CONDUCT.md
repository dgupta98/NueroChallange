# Rules, Code of Conduct & Grounds for Disqualification

---

## Official Rules

1. All teams must be **pre-formed** and composed of **3–5 teammates**
2. All teammates must have submitted individual registrations by Oct. 7 to be included
3. Teams must incorporate customer research data/strategies through a **Challenge Partner** (vetted organization from curated list)
4. Teams must create a **functional minimum viable prototype (FMVP)** incorporating AI and customer research conducted through a Challenge Partner
5. FMVP submissions must be **original work completed within the 2-week challenge sprint** — no extensions granted
6. Teams must **pitch live at a regional Launchpad by Nov. 7** — at least 2 members must be present
7. Submissions will undergo a **code review**; organizers can disqualify if code is unreadable, fraudulent, or unoriginal
8. Official submission and presentation language is **English** — all materials and presentations must be in English

---

## Grounds for Disqualification

### Partner Non-Compliance
Failing to engage with Challenge Partners or follow their rules. Teams must actively communicate with assigned Challenge Partners and strictly follow all venue, sponsor, and safety guidelines.

### Solo Participation
Working individually rather than as a team. Every entry must have two or more members. "Teams of one" are not permitted.

### Missed Deadlines
- Team Form (by 3:30 p.m. Oct. 24)
- Progress reports (daily AM/PM through Nov. 5)
- Final submission (11:59 p.m. Nov. 5)

### Code Authenticity & Originality
- Codebases must **not be entirely AI-generated**
- Teams must maintain a **"human in the loop"** who can review, explain, and validate all submitted code
- All work must be original to this event
- If substantial resemblance to a previously submitted project is found: immediate disqualification, forfeiture and revocation of any awards (even retroactively), and potential barring from future events

---

## Working with Challenge Partners

- Respect their rules — **no photos of residents or children**
- Respect communication styles and times — email during working hours, expect 24-hour response time
- Schedule through the partner's preferred platform
- Don't be late
- When in doubt, @ Challenge Organizer on Discord

---

## Teammate Code of Conduct

- **Be respectful** — value time and effort
- **Be responsive** — communicate promptly
- **Be useful** — contribute beyond ideas
- **Be reliable** — deliver on commitments

---

## General Code of Conduct

**Principles:** Respect, Inclusivity, Collaboration, Integrity

**Unacceptable Behavior:**
- Harassment (offensive comments, threats, intimidation, inappropriate contact)
- Discrimination (based on gender, sexual orientation, disability, race, religion, etc.)
- Disruption (interrupting speakers, damaging property)
- Cheating (unauthorized materials, plagiarism, violating competition rules)

**Reporting:** Report violations to event organizers immediately. All reports handled confidentially.

**Contact for violations:** oherneddo@asu.edu

**Consequences:** Warning → Expulsion → Disqualification from current and future events → Reporting to authorities if illegal
