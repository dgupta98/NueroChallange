## Arizona Autism Charter Schools (AZACS), 4125 N 14th St, Phoenix

Visited **Oct 29, AM** (Naman and team). We met a large faculty team, toured the school and met the head nurse.

| What we saw | What it means for Step Cards |
|---|---|
| About **1 staff member for every 3 students** | Teachers are hands-on but still split between 3 students who each need instructions differently. Cards cover the gap. |
| Classes in ELA, math, **coding, robotics, dance, cooking** | Lots of multi-step, hands-on tasks: ideal for step cards |
| Learning is **hands-on**. Dance and cooking build everyday skills, e.g. getting comfortable with someone being physically close | The cards should support doing, not just reading |
| Students **gave a clear recap** of what they were working on and why | Students can engage with their own progress. Self-checking fits. |
| Parents get a **daily journal** the school keeps, with worksheets stuck in, and parents check it every day | Don't replace the journal. **Feed it**: print or share the day's cards for home practice |
| Coding teaches math and English along the way | Cards can carry vocabulary at the right reading level |
| **Drone course**: learn the parts, build, fly, work toward a drone license; builds motor skills | A perfect demo routine (multi-step, precise order, safety checklist) |

Still to confirm with AZACS:
- Is following multi-step directions a daily challenge for their students?
- Would teachers use a tablet tool in class? Do students already have tablets?
- Do any students not speak? If so, pictogram-only cards matter even more.

## First Place AZ, 3001 N 3rd St, Phoenix

A supported-living apartment community for autistic and neurodivergent adults. Toured **Oct 27, AM**. Zoom with **Josh Munoz**, **Oct 29, 11:00–11:15**.

- **Transition Academy** with the "Be Open Be Ready" job-readiness curriculum, built around "360 Health & Wellness" domains (physical, social, financial, vocational, mental).
- About **1,600 activities a year**, scheduled through an app called MyClip.
- Three staff departments: Support, Community Life, Employment.
- **ADHD and autism commonly co-occur** among residents, so design for both.
- An earlier **wearable trial failed** because of sensory discomfort, so keep it app-only.
- **About half of Be Open Be Ready graduates are employed** (Josh). Use this wording. Don't say "50% lose their jobs" unless Josh confirms it.

What it means for Step Cards: the adult stage uses the same engine for recipes, chores and **work instructions**. This is where Dipesh's "pocket coach" vision lives: decoding vague manager feedback, preparing for 30/90-day reviews, and a post-shift log.

## The story across both partners

**School (AZACS) → home → independent living (First Place AZ) → first job.** One tool that grows with the person, and that needs to help less and less over time.
