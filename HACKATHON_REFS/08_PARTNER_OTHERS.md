# Challenge Partners: Kairós Inclusión, Specialisterne, Surfeando la Neurodiversidad

These three partners are Mexico-based and were presented during the Opening Ceremony Partner Breakout.

---

## Kairós Inclusión

**Website:** kairosconsultorias.com  
**Location:** Mexico  
**Contact:** Challenge Partner breakout session (Oct. 24)

### Who They Are
Promotes inclusion of people with disabilities in the **workforce and entrepreneurship** through specialized programs and comprehensive training.

**Experience:**
- 15 years in the field
- 100+ international collaborations
- 5,000+ people impacted

**Mission:** Create a truly inclusive professional ecosystem where individuals with disabilities can reach their full professional and entrepreneurial potential.

### Core Focus Areas
- Workforce inclusion (employment access for people with disabilities)
- Entrepreneurship programs for people with disabilities
- Comprehensive training and capacity building

### Pain Points / Design Opportunity
- Gap between disability and workforce entry — people with disabilities are often excluded from traditional hiring processes
- Need for specialized tools that help bridge the gap between skill-building and actual employment
- Employers need tools/processes to integrate neurodivergent employees effectively
- Entrepreneurship as an alternative path when traditional employment fails

### Design Considerations
- Target user is likely an **adult job-seeker or employed worker** with a disability
- Could also target **HR professionals or employers** as the primary user
- Mexico context — bilingual or Spanish-first UX may be important
- 15 years of data and experience = rich research partner with real case studies

## Specialisterne

**Website:** specialisternelatam.com  
**Location:** Mexico (global: founded in Denmark 2004, now in 26 countries)  
**Contact:** Challenge Partner breakout session (Oct. 24)

### Who They Are
Internationally recognized leader in harnessing the **talents of people on the autism spectrum and other neurodivergent people** — providing opportunity to sustain meaningful employment.

**Founded:** Denmark, 2004 by Thorkil Sonne  
**Scale:** 26 countries, two decades of impact  
**Approach:** Innovative hiring and management practices to enhance neurodiversity of the global workforce

**Mission:** Initiate meaningful careers and prove that different thinking can lead to exceptional achievements.

### Core Focus Areas
- Specialized neurodivergent hiring processes
- Neurodiversity-aware management practices
- Employment matching between neurodivergent talent and employers who value different thinking
- Business case for neurodiversity in the workforce

### Pain Points / Design Opportunity
- Standard hiring processes (interviews, group work, fast-paced environments) disadvantage neurodivergent candidates
- Managers lack training on how to support neurodivergent employees day-to-day
- Neurodivergent employees struggle with unwritten social rules, sudden changes, ambiguous instructions
- Executive function challenges appear at work: task initiation, switching, time management, communication

### Design Considerations
- Target user: neurodivergent professional (likely autism spectrum) navigating workplace
- Could also target employers/managers as users
- Strong employment angle — executive function challenges in professional settings
- Mexico/LATAM context but globally replicable model
- Specialisterne's global network = strong scalability story for any solution

## Surfeando la Neurodiversidad

**Website:** tqueremos.tec.mx/es/surfeando-la-neurodiversidad  
**Location:** Mexico (Tecnológico de Monterrey)  
**Contact:** Challenge Partner breakout session (Oct. 24)

### Who They Are
A Tecnológico de Monterrey program supporting neurodivergent students ("**Neurosurfers**") in their social-emotional development.

**Focus areas:** Social-emotional skills, interpersonal relationships, communication, confidence, and adaptability.

### Mission
Support the "Neurosurfer" student in managing and developing:
- Social-emotional skills
- Interpersonal relationships
- Responsibility
- Confidence and sense of security
- Adaptability
- Creativity
- Language and communication
- Problem-solving abilities

Achieved through social and academic guidance designed to help students navigate their time at the institution optimally, contributing to:
- Overall well-being
- Achievement of personal goals
- Creation of a more inclusive and empathetic community

### Pain Points / Design Opportunity
- University-level neurodivergent students face unique challenges transitioning from structured high school to less-structured university
- Social-emotional regulation is critical but often unsupported at the college level
- Communication and interpersonal challenges create isolation
- Many students struggle with confidence and self-advocacy in academic settings
- Executive function issues compound social challenges (missing deadlines, disorganization = social consequences)

### Design Considerations
- Target user: **college student** who is neurodivergent (higher functioning, enrolled in university)
- User is likely managing more independently than K–12 students but still needs support
- Mexican university context
- Strong social-emotional angle — not just academic productivity
- Peer support and community-building could be part of the solution
- Could address the transition from high school to university as a specific moment of challenge
